Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Maison Élise Nail Studio (“we”, “our”) is committed to protecting the privacy of visitors to its website, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Data collected

When you fill in our booking form or contact us, we may collect the information you provide: surname, first name, telephone number, email address and the treatment requested.

Use of data

  • To respond to your appointment and booking requests.
  • To confirm your time slots by phone or email.
  • To improve our service and the quality of our website.

We never sell your personal data to third parties.

Retention period

Personal data is kept only for the time needed to manage your booking request, then deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

In accordance with the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, delete and port your data, as well as the right to object. To exercise these rights, contact us at bonjour@maisonelise.fr.

Security

We implement reasonable measures to protect the information you entrust to us. As no transmission over the Internet is completely secure, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Contact
