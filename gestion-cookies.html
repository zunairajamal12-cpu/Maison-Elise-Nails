What are cookies?

Cookies are small files placed on your device when you visit a website. They make it possible to remember your preferences and improve your experience.

How to manage cookies?

You can at any time configure your browser to accept, refuse or delete cookies. To do so, consult the privacy settings of your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).
